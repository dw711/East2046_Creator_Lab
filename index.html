(Why)

Creativity reconnects us.

Loneliness, inequality and disconnection affect how we live. We bring artists, practitioners and communities together to improve wellbeing, strengthen belonging and reconnect people with nature and place.

(AI use)

  • MANI Engine — generated the imagined 3D garden walk from a written prompt (prompt in README).
  • Claude (Claude Code) — drafted HTML, CSS and JavaScript; the team reviewed and tested it.
  • No AI-generated people, photos, quotes or statistics. All facts come from the IRPH brief and impact report.

(Reusable workflow)

  1. Read the brief; list the claims and the three questions to answer.
  2. Keep every word, number, link and credit in one place, taken from the brief.
  3. Fact-check each claim against its source (table above).
  4. Generate the 3D world with MANI from a written prompt; test the embed.
  5. Build, then test: phone, keyboard, reduced motion, 3D skipped.
  6. Run build.py to produce one self-contained file.
